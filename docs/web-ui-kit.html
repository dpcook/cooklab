OVARIAN CANCER · CIHR 2025–2030
Why does chemotherapy stop working?
High-grade serous ovarian cancer responds to platinum chemotherapy — then relapses with disease that no longer responds. We're mapping the cell states that survive and tracking how they re-emerge.
