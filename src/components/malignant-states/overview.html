In brief

Across genetically diverse high-grade serous ovarian cancers (HGSC), malignant cells adopt recurring gene-expression states. These states are linked to a cell's location in the tumour, to patient survival and to response to bevacizumab, a therapy that blocks VEGF signalling.

1 · Single-cell atlas

One axis

Malignant secretory cells vary along the same axis across tumours, from SecA to SecB

We combined nearly 2 million single-cell transcriptomes from 13 published studies, spanning 362 samples from 158 patients before and after chemotherapy. Samples covered 12 anatomical sites, including primary tumours, metastases and ascites, the fluid that accumulates in the abdominal cavity.

Within the epithelial compartment, we identified two opposing gene-expression programs in secretory cancer cells. Secretory A (SecA) expresses developmental and progenitor-associated genes, including MECOM and SOX17. Secretory B (SecB) expresses keratins and genes associated with mucosal injury, including KRT7, KRT19 and LCN2.

SecA and SecB mark the ends of a continuum, with intermediate cells between them. We call a cell's position along this continuum its polarization: higher scores indicate a stronger SecB program relative to SecA.

Polarization was the leading pattern of malignant-cell gene expression in 54% of tumour samples and one of the three leading patterns in 82%. It was distinct from programs associated with the cell cycle, stress, interferon and epithelial–mesenchymal transition (EMT).

2 · Cell states

Two programs

SecA cells proliferate; SecB cells are quiescent and stress-adapted

Signalling and the cell cycle

SecA cells show stronger growth-associated programs, including MYC, E2F and MAPK; 37.8% are in S or G2/M phase. SecB cells show stronger hypoxia and stress-response programs and a greater inferred reliance on glycolysis; 80.6% are classified as G1.

SecB dominates ascites

Before treatment, SecB made up 68% of epithelial cells in ascites, against 14% in primary tumours and 17% in solid metastases. In 14 patients sampled at several sites, the median was 60.7% in ascites and 10.2% in the primary tumour.

SecB accounted for 24% of epithelial cells in post-chemotherapy primary tumours, compared with 14% before treatment. In the separate analysis of 18 patients with matched pre- and post-treatment samples, its share increased in 12, although the paired difference was not statistically significant (p = 0.11).

3 · Spatial transcriptomics

Architecture

SecB cells are concentrated in hypoxic regions farther from blood vessels

We mapped where these states occur within tissue using a 477-gene Xenium panel tailored to ovarian cancer. We profiled eight whole-tumour sections, 503 tumour tissue-microarray cores from 378 patients, and 15 healthy fallopian-tube tissue cores: about 3.4 million cells in all. The gradient across tumour sections

Across more than 1.2 million secretory cells in the eight tumour sections, higher SecB polarization was associated with greater distance from blood vessels, lower local cell density, and higher hypoxia and matrix-remodelling gene-expression scores.

The same holds in the tissue microarrays: across 361 patients, SecB cells were farther from the nearest vessel than SecA cells (p < 0.0001).

4 · The niche

A shared niche

SecB cells and nearby macrophages share hypoxia-associated programs in regions with fewer B cells

Toward the SecB end of the continuum, secretory cells show lower expression of proliferation, growth and DNA-repair programs, alongside higher glycolysis and TNF/NF-κB signalling.

The cells around them differ too. Across the gradient from lower to higher local hypoxia, macrophages make up a larger share of non-epithelial cells (13.4% at the low end, 32.6% at the high end) and B cells a smaller one (3.8% and 0.7%), in all eight tumour sections. T cells and plasma cells trend lower.

Macrophages show a similar program

Macrophages in SecB regions show higher expression of glycolysis and NF-κB genes and lower expression of tissue-resident markers. Visium HD profiling of WT01, the tumour shown above, also identified a hypoxia-associated SPP1+ MMP9+ macrophage state beside SecB regions.

Potential signals within the niche

Gene expression points to possible interactions within this niche. Macrophages express VEGFA, while nearby SecB cells show higher expression of the NF-κB target BIRC3. Other genes suggest potential roles in immune protection, lactate export, adhesion and matrix remodelling. Together, these patterns support a model in which SecB cells and macrophages form cohesive groups that could detach into ascites.

5 · Experimental models

Missing in models

Patient-derived organoids favour SecA, and none of the conditions we tested fully restored SecB

We profiled eight patient-derived organoid lines by single-nucleus RNA-seq. Fewer than 1% of organoid cells reached the SecB threshold of the atlas. Lines grown from ascites kept higher SecB scores than lines from primary tumours (p = 0.029), and in one line SecB scores were lower after 6–12 days in culture than at day 2.

Of four microenvironmental signals tested, only IFN-γ increased SecB gene expression, and none of the culture media restored the full state. Published organoid and spheroid datasets captured only parts of SecB: hypoxic cultures reproduced the HIF response but retained high proliferation. The KPCA.B mouse model also captured some features, including a Krt7+ luminal population, without a gene-expression program matching human SecB.

6 · Clinical outcome

Outcome

Greater SecB polarization is associated with poorer survival and greater bevacizumab benefit

Among 104 treatment-naive patients represented by two tumour cores, those in the most SecB-enriched third had worse 5-year overall survival than those in the most SecA-dominant third (log-rank p = 0.011). Polarization remained prognostic after adjusting for stage, residual disease and age (HR 1.51 per standard deviation).

The survival association also held in 314 TCGA-OV tumours scored by bulk RNA-seq. Higher polarization was associated with worse overall survival and progression-free survival after adjustment for epithelial content, stage and age (adjusted HRs 1.84 and 1.65, respectively).

Bevacizumab in ICON7

Bevacizumab benefit was greater in SecB-polarized tumours

SecB niches are distant from blood vessels and contain macrophages expressing VEGFA. We therefore asked whether polarization was associated with benefit from bevacizumab. We scored pretreatment tumours from 191 patients with stage III/IV disease in ICON7 and divided them at the median polarization score. Within each group, we compared carboplatin–paclitaxel alone with carboplatin–paclitaxel plus bevacizumab.

In SecB-polarized tumours, median progression-free survival was 18.7 months with bevacizumab versus 11.1 months with chemotherapy alone (HR 0.36). The treatment effect differed between the polarization groups (interaction p = 0.025); no statistically significant benefit was detected in the SecA group. In the analysis split at 15 months, the estimated benefit in SecB tumours was strongest during the first 15 months (HR 0.23), with no clear benefit detected afterwards (HR 0.87).

7 · What it means

Implications

A recurring cell state offers a way to investigate relapse, test treatment selection and develop practical biomarkers

Relapse
Could SecB cells seed recurrence?

SecB cells show lower proliferation and occupy regions distant from blood vessels. Their share increased after chemotherapy in 12 of 18 patients with matched samples, although the paired difference was not statistically significant. These observations raise the possibility that SecB cells persist through treatment and contribute to recurrence, a hypothesis for longitudinal studies.

Selection
A candidate marker of bevacizumab benefit

Our retrospective ICON7 analysis links SecB polarization to greater progression-free survival benefit from bevacizumab. Prospective validation is needed to establish whether it can guide treatment selection. Whether polarization can also inform treatment duration remains a separate question.

Measurement
Pathology markers could make testing practical

KRT7 and KRT19 protein levels correlated with the polarization score measured by Xenium in the same tissue cores; E-cadherin, KRT18 and vimentin did not. These markers provide a starting point for a practical tissue-based test. Serum CYFRA 21-1, a KRT19 fragment, is another candidate to investigate as a blood-based readout.
